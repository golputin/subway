Questions

FAQ

Do I need a token to play?

No. Anyone can play the free demo instantly. Holding $HOOD unlocks Compete mode, which makes your scores eligible for the prize pool.

Where can I buy $HOOD?

$HOOD launches on the Pons Launchpad on Robinhood Chain as a fair-launch bonding curve. When it's live, the “Buy on Pons” button opens its token page. Early buyers get in on the curve before graduation.

What is “graduation”?

On Pons, a token trades on a bonding curve until it reaches a market-cap threshold. At that point it graduates: its liquidity moves to a DEX and locks, making it freely tradable. That's when Compete-mode token gating switches on.

How do payouts work?

Compete scores are ranked on the leaderboard. Once the backend and treasury are live, the top slots share the USDT pool. Until then, this build is a playable MVP — scores are stored locally and are not yet a source of truth for real payouts.

Which wallets are supported?

Any injected EVM wallet (MetaMask and compatible). Connect once and your address becomes your profile.

Is it heavy on my device?

No — it's built with lightweight WebGL and runs in the browser on phones and laptops. No install required.
