Subway Hood Docs

Subway Hood is a lightweight Web3 subway runner: dash down the tracks, dodge trains, stack coins, and climb a global leaderboard to earn from a USDT prize pool. Play free in Practice, or hold $5 worth of $SBWY to Compete for rewards.

Game & site: live   Leaderboard API: live   $SBWY token: coming soon

Quick start

  1. Open the game and hit Play — no wallet needed for Practice.
  2. Connect an EVM wallet (MetaMask or compatible) to bind your profile to your address.
  3. Hold at least $5 worth of $SBWY to unlock Compete — now your scores are recorded.
  4. Rack up your best run. The leaderboard splits the pool.

Gameplay & controls

Three lanes. You run automatically — your job is to survive as long as possible. Speed ramps up over time; oncoming trains rush toward you.

Avoid (tall)Trains, barriers, signal boxes → change lane
Jump over (low)Cones, crates → jump
ScoreDistance travelled + coins collected. One crash = game over.

Modes & eligibility

Practice

Free for everyone, no wallet or token required. You can play the full game — but Practice scores are never recorded and are not eligible for the pool.

Compete

Unlocks when your wallet holds at least $5 worth of $SBWY — any token amount is fine as long as it's worth that much. In Compete, each run is validated by the server and recorded on the live leaderboard.

Eligibility is by USD value of your holdings ($5), not a fixed token count — so it stays fair as the price moves.

$SBWY & the Pons launch

$SBWY is the game token. It launches on the Pons Launchpad on Robinhood Chain as a fair-launch bonding curve — no presale and no team allocation. Everyone buys on the same curve.

  • Fair launch — 100% of supply goes to the bonding curve.
  • Bonding curve — price rises as people buy; get in early before graduation.
  • Graduation → DEX — once the curve fills, liquidity migrates to a DEX and locks, and Compete gating goes fully live.
When the token is live, the Buy on Pons button on the home page links straight to its Pons token page.

Prize pool & rewards

The current pool is 5,000 USDT. Rewards are not winner-takes-all — every eligible player earns a share.

  • Pro-rata by basis points. Your share = your points ÷ total points, expressed in basis points (1 bps = 0.01%).
  • 2-day (48h) cooldown. Distributions run at most once every 48 hours.
  • Paid from the treasury. The backend computes each address's bps; USDT is paid out from the project treasury.

Example: if you produced 3% of all points in a cycle, you'd receive ~3% (300 bps) of that cycle's distributed pool.

Leaderboard & anti-cheat

The leaderboard is served by a live backend. Every competitive run is checked before it's accepted:

  • Signed session per run — one-time token issued at the start of each run.
  • Server-measured duration — the client's timing is never trusted.
  • Plausibility checks — score can't exceed the max physically possible for that run length; coin counts are bounded.
  • Rate limits per IP, and a locked CORS origin.
Roadmap: heuristics deter casual cheating but aren't bulletproof. Planned upgrades: server-side on-chain balance verification for Compete, and replay validation (re-simulating a run from its input log + seed) for full integrity.

FAQ

Do I need a token to play?

No — Practice is free. Holding $5 worth of $SBWY unlocks Compete, where scores count toward the pool.

Which wallets are supported?

Any injected EVM wallet (MetaMask and compatible).

Is it heavy on my device?

No — it's lightweight WebGL and runs in the browser on phones and laptops. No install.

Can I hide the game's API requests from DevTools?

No. Any request a browser makes is visible in the Network tab — that's how browsers work. Security comes from server-side validation, not from hiding URLs.
